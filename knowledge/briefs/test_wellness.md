# Wellness Brand Campaign

## Campaign Goal
Build awareness for a premium wellness supplement line targeting health-conscious millennials and Gen Z. Focus on authentic, relatable creators who embody a balanced lifestyle.

## Target Audience
Health-conscious women and men aged 22–35, interested in fitness, nutrition, and mental wellness. Budget-conscious but willing to invest in quality products. Active on Instagram, value authenticity and educational content.

## Niche
Health & Wellness, Fitness

## Creator Tier
micro (20k–100k)

## Location
United Kingdom, United States, Canada

## Minimum Engagement Rate
2.5%

## Content Style
Educational, relatable fitness/wellness content. Mix of workout clips, nutrition tips, morning routines, mental health discussions. Authentic, not overly polished. Prefer creators with engaged communities and genuine product recommendations.

## Brand Safety Notes
Avoid creators who heavily promote competing supplement brands. Avoid extreme diet culture or unsafe fitness claims. Prefer creators with genuine wellness credentials or certifications.
