## How I evaluate creators for KLORIS

KLORIS is a premium wellness brand. The bar is higher than the average client — they pivoted away from price-mismatched audiences in April 2026 and explicitly told us they want trust-led creators, not deal-led ones. When in doubt, lean strict.

**What makes a great fit (9–10/10):**
- Female creator, 35+, UK-based (EU acceptable, see brief notes)
- Has clear editorial credibility — a point of view, a beat, a craft. Podcast hosts, writers, dancers, doctors, equestrians, gardeners, credentialed practitioners. Not a generic "lifestyle/wellness" review account
- Audience reads premium — engagement looks intentional (long comments, real conversations), not bot-like, and the niche signals disposable income (corporate, country, equestrian, nature/garden, mid-career professional)
- Aesthetic skews calm, considered, slow-living. Sleep, recovery, quiet ritual, evening routines, nature, books. Not gym-bro fitness, not deal-hunting hauls, not chronic-illness advocacy
- Genuine product use is plausible — Calm & Glow Face Oil, Sleep Patches, Sleep Mist, Discovery Set all fit naturally into their content world
- Real-world precedent: @emmajanegallagher (their strongest on-brand creator), @julieyourfeelgoodgirl, @evelynscott1987 (86k, organic fan), @katherinemay_, @alicevincentwrites, @thejenniferwalsh. If a candidate would sit comfortably next to these, that's a strong signal

**Deal-breakers (instant disqualify):**
- Male creators or creators under 25 — out of brief unless specifically a doctor/credentialed niche carve-out
- Outside UK/EU — out of brief unless explicitly flagged
- Chronic Pain or Endometriosis niche as primary identity — actively being phased out by client decision; audience price mismatch
- "Busy Mum" niche where audience reads budget/coupon-driven rather than premium
- Pure product-review accounts — affiliate code in every caption, no personal POV
- Deal-led aesthetic — discount codes, sale alerts, "I got this for free" energy
- MLM involvement (anything Herbalife / Arbonne / Optavia / Beachbody-adjacent)
- Anti-vax, anti-medication, pseudo-medical claims — KLORIS is health-adjacent, can't risk it
- Adult content, OnlyFans links, sexualised aesthetic — universal hard reject
- Hate speech, conspiracy content, political extremism — universal hard reject

**How I weight signals:**
- *Trust signal / editorial credibility* — most important. KLORIS told us this directly. Does the creator have a craft, a point of view, a reason their audience trusts them? Score this above everything else.
- *Audience quality* — second-most. Engagement rate matters less than engagement *texture* — what do comments actually look like? Are they conversations or emoji strings? An audience of 12k engaged corporate women > 200k generic wellness followers.
- *Aesthetic fit / niche match* — third. Sleep / recovery / nature / equestrian / corporate-stress all sit nicely. Hustle culture and bro-fitness do not.
- *Engagement rate (numeric)* — useful sanity check, not a primary signal. A 1% engagement rate on the right creator (mid-career professional with 50k followers, mostly stories) can still be a strong yes.
- *Production quality* — minimum bar required (must look presentable, not phone-flash selfies in cluttered rooms). Above that bar, doesn't really matter. KLORIS isn't running paid ads off this content (yet).
- *Posting frequency / recency* — must be active. Stale accounts (30+ days dormant) are a hard pass — KLORIS wants creators who'll actually post.

**Size preference:**
- 5k–500k follower range covers most of the brief
- Sweet spot: 20k–150k where engagement and audience-fit are richest
- Will accept larger (500k+) if editorial credibility is exceptional (e.g. @katherinemay_, established author/podcast host)
- Will accept smaller (sub-10k) if highly niche-aligned with engaged community

**Content styles that work:**
- Day-in-the-life with calm wind-down rituals (face oil, sleep mist, journaling)
- Recovery / sleep-hygiene-focused educational content
- Equestrian and country lifestyle (stables, gardens, slow weekends)
- Corporate-professional unwinding (post-work, weekend reset)
- Nature, gardening, hiking, slow travel
- Doctor / practitioner explainers — but only if natural / non-shouty
- Menopause-focused content from credentialed or lived-experience creators (this niche is being actively expanded)
- Long-form story posts where they actually talk about how a product fits their life

**Content styles that don't work:**
- Pure unboxing / haul / affiliate-code-stuffed captions
- "What I got in PR" review accounts
- Loud fitness content (HIIT shouting, before-and-after weight loss)
- Coupon / discount / "I'm a deal-hunter" energy
- Hyper-aesthetic content with no spoken POV (mood-board accounts)
- Drama / callout / cancellation culture
- Chronic illness advocacy as primary content identity (despite product fit, audience is wrong commercially)

**Notes for the agent:**
When in doubt on a Kloris creator, default to NO rather than maybe. The client gave us very specific feedback in April 2026 — they want a tighter roster, not a bigger one. Every yes you put forward should be defensible against the question "would Pedram see this account and immediately get it?" If you can't picture them nodding, hold the creator back.

The single sharpest test: does this creator have an audience whose default reaction to a £40 sleep mist is "ooh, lovely" rather than "where's the discount code?" If the answer is the second, it's a no for KLORIS — even if every other signal is green.
